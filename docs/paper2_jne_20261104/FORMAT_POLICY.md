## Journal-specific requirements

The target article is a **Paper** (official category: “Papers”). Normal length guidance is **12,000 words / 14 journal pages**; this is not an absolute 14-page limit on the review PDF. The abstract must use **Objective, Approach, Main results, Significance**. JNE allows either single-anonymous or double-anonymous review. This package retains author information for single-anonymous review. Conflicts must be disclosed in the cover letter and article acknowledgements. Scope includes brain–computer interfaces and neural signal processing; the scope statement asks computational classification work for robust comparisons, validation and explanatory insight. Formatting alone does not establish editorial suitability. [JNE scope and special requirements](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/journal-of-neural-engineering/about-journal-neural-engineering/)

## Initial manuscript and references

Submit a **complete PDF with figures and tables embedded near their discussion**, plus relevant separate supplements. IOP accepts free-format initial submissions; official Word/LaTeX templates are optional. Use readable **≥12 pt** text and reasonable spacing. Author-added line numbers are unnecessary because the submission system adds them. Editable source files remain available for production. [Article format](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/article-format/)

Keep the informative title, full author names/affiliations, correspondence details and relevant keywords. ORCIDs are recommended. The abstract should normally be **≤300 words**, self-contained, with abbreviations defined and no citations, equations or figure/table references. Organize the research as Introduction, Method, Results, Discussion and Conclusion; acknowledgements precede references and include funding and conflicts. Tables need sequential numbers/captions and no colour shading/text. No fixed keyword count, title-character limit or mandatory running head was verified. [Article structure](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/structure-and-format-of-your-journal-article/)

The existing **Vancouver numbered square-bracket** system is acceptable; list in citation order and use one system consistently. Harvard is also allowed. Exact house formatting is not mandatory at initial submission; verify bibliographic facts and cite original persistent records. No JNE reference-count cap was verified. [References](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/references/)

## Figures, supplementary files and sources

Prefer vector **PDF/EPS**; TIFF/PNG/JPEG are accepted. Aim for **8–12 pt** text at final size (typical widths **8.5/15 cm**), accessible non-colour-only encoding and **(a), (b)** panel labels. Put captions in the article. Keep separate figure files where possible. No numerical dpi threshold was verified; 600 dpi TIFF is a preparation choice. Supplements: **≤50 MB each**, **≤150 MB combined including main article**, internal title **≤30 characters**, internal description **≤30 words**. Material essential to the argument belongs in the article. Editable Word equations/TeX and source ZIPs are supported. Use simple filenames with numbered figures. [JNE author guidelines, Figures / Supplementary material / Source files](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/journal-of-neural-engineering/)

## Statements and transparency

A **data availability statement** is required. Give actual dataset/code/result locations or explain real restrictions. An archived DOI copy of GitHub material is recommended, rather than a universal initial-submission gate. Cite data/software accurately, including persistent identifiers where available; do not invent a DOI. [Research data policy](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/iop-publishing-data-availability-policy/)

Place the study’s accurate statutory ethics basis in Methods, separately from approval and consent for the original collection. Material AI assistance requires a **separate Acknowledgements disclosure** naming tools, tasks and author verification; AI used in research additionally needs Methods detail. Grammar-only use is exempt, but code/analysis assistance is broader. Authors retain responsibility. The confirmed AI-use scope must not be narrowed to grammar alone. [Ethical policy, Generative AI / Ethical statements](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/ethical-policy-journals/)
