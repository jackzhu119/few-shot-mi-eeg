## Novelty & Significance

We quantify Euclidean alignment’s incremental contribution after supervised CSP/LDA refitting under matched calibration information. In Kumar2024 and BNCI2014-001, alignment added 5.30 and 5.65 balanced-accuracy points, respectively, and improved weak-class recall. Participant-level prediction transitions reveal corrected and introduced errors. Shared-coordinate, scaling and covariance controls examine the computational contribution, while paired Kumar cue/task-window analyses establish its signal-definition dependence. The study connects calibration operations to longitudinal performance and class coverage, providing a detailed account of what a short calibration run contributes.
