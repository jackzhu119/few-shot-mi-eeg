# Ethics statement

This study was a secondary analysis of publicly available EEG data from the Kumar2024 dataset, obtained from NEMAR (nm000177, version 1.0.5). Records were provided under coded participant identifiers. No new participants were recruited or contacted, no interventions were performed, and no attempts were made to re-identify participants. The author had no access to information that could identify individual participants during or after data collection.

The original study reported ethical approval from the University of Texas at Austin (protocol 2020-03-0073) and written informed consent obtained before data collection. These statements refer to the original data collection.

The present secondary analysis was exempt from additional ethical review under Article 32, item (1), of China's Measures for Ethical Review of Life Science and Medical Research Involving Human Participants (2023). It used lawfully obtained publicly available data, caused no harm to participants, and did not involve sensitive personal information or commercial interests.

Dataset metadata were accessed on 10 October 2026, and the selected EEG recordings and accompanying metadata were retrieved on 11 October 2026.

Regulatory source: National Health Commission and the co-issuing departments, Guo Wei Ke Jiao Fa [2023] No. 4, Article 32, item (1). Official text: https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2023-02/28/content_5743658.htm
