# Figure captions

**Fig 5. Class-balanced error transitions separate correction from newly introduced errors.** Rows show the predefined primary cohort (12 participants), development cohort (6), and secondary all-participant summary (18). The contrasts are LDA-only recalibration minus frozen decoding, full refitting minus LDA-only recalibration, and full refitting minus frozen decoding. In A, C and E, blue bars show contributions from baseline errors corrected by the updated model; hatched orange bars show newly introduced errors with negative sign. Each rate uses the full same-class query denominator before averaging the two classes, five sessions and participants equally. Their sum equals the BA change. Black diamonds and bars show net BA changes and descriptive 95% participant-bootstrap intervals. B, D and F show left- and right-class recall changes with the same participant weighting and bootstrap method (10,000 resamples; seed 42). The all-18 row includes the other two cohorts and is not an independent replication. These decompositions were computed from saved predictions after observation of the original results.

**Fig 6. Joint component gains and target-session profiles.** (A) Each point is one participant's equally weighted mean over five target sessions. The horizontal coordinate is LDA-only minus frozen BA; the vertical coordinate is full-refit minus LDA-only BA. Numbers identify the published participant IDs. Circles show the primary cohort and open squares the development cohort. The dashed line marks zero total full-refit-minus-frozen gain. (B) Primary-cohort session means for the total refit contrast and the same-label component contrast. Bars are descriptive 95% participant-bootstrap intervals (12 participants; 10,000 resamples; seed 42); the same participants appear in all sessions. Session profiles are exploratory descriptions, without separate session-level hypothesis tests. (C) Primary-cohort mean changes after leaving out each indicated participant (11 participants per point). Dashed lines mark the original 12-participant means; leave-one-out points are dependent influence summaries, not confidence limits or validation folds. Exact values for all three cohorts are supplied in the source-data tables.
