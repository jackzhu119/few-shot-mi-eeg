# Suggested reviewer sources

The names, institutional affiliations and publicly listed professional contact details below were checked against the researchers' own professional pages or university/research-institute pages on 28 October 2026. Country means the location of the listed institution, not the reviewer's nationality. The five candidates are based at institutions outside Chongqing Medical University.

The author confirmed in this chat that, to the author's knowledge, none of these five candidates has a coauthorship, supervisory, collaborative or other relevant conflict with either author. Accordingly, `author_confirmed_no_known_conflict` in `Suggested_Reviewers.csv` records the author's confirmation; it is not an independent certification of reviewer independence. No candidate was contacted, and no agreement to review is claimed. The journal determines reviewer selection and eligibility.
