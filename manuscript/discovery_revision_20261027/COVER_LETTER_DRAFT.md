# Cover letter — author-review draft

Dear Professor Haueisen,

Please consider my Research Article, “Matched-label analysis identifies spatial refitting and alignment gains in motor-imagery EEG,” for Biomedical Engineering / Biomedizinische Technik.

The study quantifies the additional value of spatial refitting and support-only alignment when a new recording session provides approximately 20 labels. In the predefined 12-participant cohort, full common-spatial-pattern/linear-discriminant-analysis refitting added 5.47 percentage points of balanced accuracy beyond classifier-only recalibration (95% participant-bootstrap interval 2.17–8.91). It accounted for 73.7% of the total mean improvement over frozen decoding. Trial-level prediction transitions and longitudinal participant analyses explain this component contribution.

In the secondary comparison in nine BNCI2014-001 participants, support-only EA-CSP improved on full refitting by 5.65 points (3.15–8.70; Holm-adjusted p=0.01172), with positive differences in all nine participants. Full refitting and classifier-only recalibration differed by +0.19 points (95% interval −1.85 to 2.13).

Exploratory experiments in the six development participants extended the comparison to neural normalization. At the 20-label cap, weight adaptation with fixed source normalization achieved 1.50–1.91 percentage points higher balanced accuracy than adaptive normalization across two source-model regimes. The complete budget profiles show how this effect varies with calibration size.

The manuscript fits the journal's biosignal-processing and neuro-engineering scope. Public data provenance, the regulatory basis for secondary use, code and data availability, and substantive AI assistance are documented in the separate ethical and legal declarations.
