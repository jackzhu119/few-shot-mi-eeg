# Cover letter — final author-review draft

Dear Professor Haueisen,

Please consider our Research Article, “Disentangling spatial-filter and classifier contributions to limited-label motor-imagery EEG recalibration”, for Biomedical Engineering / Biomedizinische Technik.

The manuscript addresses how a short calibration run should change a motor-imagery decoder. By giving classifier-only recalibration, full spatial refitting and support-only alignment the same source and current-session labels, we quantify the contribution of each update operation on identical query trials. In the original twelve-participant Kumar cohort, spatial refitting added 5.47 balanced-accuracy points beyond classifier recalibration and accounted for 73.7% of the total mean improvement over freezing. Prediction transitions and five-session participant summaries explain that increment.

The completed calibration-budget analysis adds a second spatial operation. Support-only alignment increased balanced accuracy by 5.30 points beyond full refitting in Kumar (pointwise 95% interval 2.81–8.26) and by 5.65 points in the separately fixed nine-participant BNCI comparison (3.15–8.70; secondary Holm p=0.01172). All nine external participants improved. Full versus classifier-only refitting in BNCI differed by 0.19 points (−1.85 to 2.13). Complete budget curves, marker-aware window sensitivity and the full neural/geometric controls accompany these comparisons.

The contribution is a reproducible analysis of where limited-label recalibration gains arise, supported by shared trial identities and participant-level estimates. The study uses our recorded analyses of public datasets; source methods and acquisition studies receive their appropriate citations. Data/code availability, national-law secondary-use ethics and actual AI assistance are documented in the separate declarations.
