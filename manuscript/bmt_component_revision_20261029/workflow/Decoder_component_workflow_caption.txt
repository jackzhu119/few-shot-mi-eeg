Four decoder actions under the recorded source and current-session support partitions. Frozen CSP/LDA is fitted to source training alone. LDA-only keeps the source CSP fixed and refits LDA on the source/support union. Full refitting refits both CSP and LDA on that same union. EA estimates separate arithmetic covariance references from source training and current support, aligns each domain and fits CSP/LDA on the aligned union. Its later query is transformed using the already fixed support-derived target reference. Query signals and labels do not estimate references or parameters or select a method. Source training contains 40 labels in Kumar2024 and 96 in BNCI2014-001; current support contains 19 or 20 and 20 labels, respectively. The three updating pipelines share source/support identities within each dataset; frozen uses zero target labels. Dataset-specific preprocessing, coordinates and covariance estimators are specified in the Methods. This diagram depicts existing operations and introduces no new algorithm or experiment.
