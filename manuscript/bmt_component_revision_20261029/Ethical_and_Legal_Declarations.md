## Research ethics

The present secondary analysis was exempt from additional ethical review under Article 32, item (1), of China's Measures for Ethical Review of Life Science and Medical Research Involving Human Participants (2023). It used lawfully obtained publicly available data, caused no harm to participants, and did not involve sensitive personal information or commercial interests. The original Kumar data collection reported University of Texas at Austin approval under protocol 2020-03-0073. The reviewed BNCI2014-001 source publication and dataset description did not supply a dataset-2a-specific committee, approval number or consent statement; their data-collection ethics details are not inferred from another dataset. Ziyuan Zhu had no access to identifying participant information and made no attempt to re-identify participants.

## Informed consent

The original Kumar study reported written informed consent before data collection. No dataset-2a-specific consent statement was identified in the reviewed BNCI source documents. The present study analyzed coded public records; no new participants were recruited or contacted and no additional consent was collected.
