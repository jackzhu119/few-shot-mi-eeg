# Classical-method analysis of saved V2 development results

Scope: six previously exposed Kumar participants, five target sessions, fixed support caps4/8/12/16/20 (the maximum cap yields19–20 labels), eight classical arms. No model fit, EEG read or forward inference was performed. All interval calculations retain the frozen10,000 participant-bootstrap draws and seed20261023, using the original lexicographic participant order. Sessions/query trials are not treated as independent people. Additional session slices and influence checks are descriptive and post hoc; the primary endpoint remains the originally declared cap20 EA+empirical anchoring minus EA-CSP contrast.

## What the data support

1. **The new empirical anchoring rule does not establish an advantage over EA-CSP.** At cap20 it achieves69.24% BA versus70.37%, a paired difference of−1.14pp [−3.52,+1.14]. Three participants improve and three decline; sub-7 declines5.67pp. Removing any single participant keeps the mean difference negative (−1.93 to−0.23pp), so this finding is not explained solely by one poor participant. This is not an equivalence test or proof of no possible benefit.
2. **Supervised recalibration still helps relative to frozen source decoding.** The candidate minus frozen is+7.09pp [+4.12,+11.55], positive for all six participants, range+3.17 to+17.72pp. The largest gain is sub-4; after omitting that participant, the remaining mean is still+4.96pp. Other leave-one-out means range up to+7.88pp. Full CSP/LDA minus frozen is+6.50pp [+3.24,+9.39] in an additional descriptive summary. These refer to the new six-person V2 implementation, not the manuscript's historical remaining12 endpoint.
3. **Candidate versus unaligned full CSP has mixed individual effects.** The mean is+0.59pp [−4.77,+6.52], but only sub-4 (+12.61pp) and sub-16 (+7.99pp) improve; four participants decline and two decline at least5pp. Its leave-one-out mean changes sign (−1.82 to+2.07pp). A positive cohort point estimate here should not be described as broadly reliable superiority.
4. **The fixed-weight control offers no evidence that estimating alpha is beneficial.** Empirical minus fixed-tau anchoring after the same EA is−1.11pp [−4.27,+2.07]. Candidate minus its unaligned empirical ablation is+3.86pp [−0.61,+8.88]. These do not isolate a statistically reliable cap20 benefit of empirical weighting or alignment. The latter estimate is consistent with a useful alignment effect but remains imprecise.
5. **The data do not justify selecting16 labels as a newly optimal budget.** Candidate/EA mean BA by cap is66.08/66.79 (4),68.18/67.48 (8),68.39/67.71 (12),70.64/70.86 (16),69.24/70.37 (20). All five candidate-minus-EA intervals cross zero. The apparent decline from16 to20 is an observed development fluctuation, not a frozen stopping rule. More labels alter individual support composition; a monotonic finite-sample performance guarantee was never assumed.
6. **The longitudinal average hides early/late differences.** At cap20, candidate-minus-EA session means are−1.93,−6.32,−1.23,+2.92,+0.88pp. Candidate-minus-frozen means are−0.14,−0.37,+4.43,+18.49,+13.06pp. Frozen BA is64.41,68.25,64.91,55.94,57.22%, whereas candidate BA is64.26,67.87,69.34,74.43,70.28%. Thus the average benefit over frozen is concentrated later; these observational slices cannot establish a physiological drift mechanism. Across the30 participant×session cells, candidate versus frozen declines in8 (6 by≥5pp), despite all six five-session participant means being positive. Candidate versus EA declines in14 cells (10 by≥5pp).
7. **Spatial refitting has uncertain incremental value over LDA-only within this V2 development sample.** Full CSP minus LDA-only is+1.35pp [−1.57,+4.99], an explicitly additional descriptive contrast. EA-CSP minus unaligned full refit is+1.72pp [−1.88,+5.76]. Neither is a replacement for the historical manuscript endpoint or the V2 primary contrast.

## Precision and interpretation

All reported intervals are pointwise exploratory participant intervals with no multiplicity correction. Six exposed participants give limited precision; there is no new unseen cohort. A session-specific interval excluding zero, such as the cap20 candidate-minus-EA deficit in session2, remains a post hoc slice and should not become a new selector or primary claim. A≥5pp decrease is the prespecified descriptive magnitude threshold, not a clinical harm threshold. The5-second windows and prior task-alignment limitations remain unchanged.

The scientific implication is narrower than a successful new adaptation algorithm: supervised recalibration gives meaningful gains versus freezing, but the specific support-noise shrinkage rule has not demonstrated extra value after the existing strong alignment comparator. The frozen primary contrast, negative results and complete individual/longitudinal distributions should remain visible in any later replication.
