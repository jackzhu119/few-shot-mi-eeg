## Data and code availability

The source EEG dataset is publicly available as the NEMAR release
nm000177, version 1.0.5 (https://doi.org/10.82901/nemar.nm000177.v1.0.5),
under the provider's CC BY 4.0 terms. The associated original study is
Kumar et al. (https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae076); its published
correction (https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae137) is also cited.
Source recordings were not recollected or modified for public redistribution
by the present analysis. Trial identities, labels, fixed quality flags, saved
predictions and the participant/session tables underlying the reported findings
are provided in the paper-specific minimal-data archive and its byte-level
manifest. Analysis code, frozen configurations and reproduction instructions
are maintained at https://github.com/jackzhu119/few-shot-mi-eeg on the
research/learning-preserving-bci branch. The paper-package manifest identifies
the fixed Git version and exact paths for the submitted analysis. Private
preservation copies are not the public-access route. No new archive DOI is
claimed. The final software/content license selection remains with the author.
