36   The channel order was F7, F3, Fz, F4, F8, FC5, FC1, FC2, FC6, C3, Cz, C4, CP5, CP1, CP2, CP6,

38   was in volts; the native physical metadata used microvolts. Each epoch independently underwent:


39      1. Common-average reference over all 22 channels at each sample.

40      2. Fourth-order SciPy Butterworth SOS band-pass filtering at 8–30 Hz with sosfiltfilt and

41         its default odd padding.

42      3. resample_poly(up=1, down=4) with its default Kaiser-5 window and constant padding,

43         yielding 22 × 640 float32 values at 128 Hz in volts.


44   These operations fitted no state across epochs. Zero-phase filtering is an offline convention. A raw

45   within-window peak-to-peak value above 250 µV in any channel generated a fixed quality flag. All

46   1,512 flagged complete windows remained among the 7,156 primary windows. No expert artifact

47   certification or verified reconstruction of the acquisition CPz reference was claimed.


48   The predefined power description used Welch estimates with a Hann window, 256-sample segments,

49   128-sample overlap, constant detrending, density scaling, one-sided output and mean averaging.

51   edge bins. Features were log10(max(power, float64_tiny)), ordered channel-major with Mu

53   not baseline-normalized ERD.

116   dependent people. The six development participants and 12 primary participants were analyzed

121   maries used lexicographic participant-ID order. The six-person reliability bootstrap used numeric

166   S5.3. Frozen SOURCE40 coordinates and signed scores

167   Current support used the six existing SOURCE40 baseline CSP/LDA models without a new decoder

168   fit. For an exported cue-anchored window X, the four CSP features were the natural logarithms of

169   mean squared projected amplitude:


170   f_j(X) = ln(mean_t((W_j X_t)^2)), j=1,...,4.


171   The executed consumer rejected nonpositive/nonfinite power rather than inventing features. The

172   vector diagnostic fitted a four-coordinate StandardScaler on SOURCE40 features only, using popu-

173   lation variance (ddof=0), and applied that fixed transform to support. Its fixed source direction was

174   d_source = mean(z|right) − mean(z|left) and its unit direction was u_source = d_source

175   / ||d_source|| when estimable.

176   Within each support class, trials were chronologically ordered by acquisition time, start sample and

177   source row. The first floor(n_class/2) entered the first half and the remainder entered the sec-

178   ond; corresponding class halves were combined. For a half direction d_h, the diagnostics retained

179   source/full support cosine, half-to-half cosine and the signed axis contrast


180   A_h = (d_h · u_source) / SD_SOURCE40(z · u_source).


181   This contrast has no factor of one half. Its source projection SD used ddof=1. Zero directions,

182   missing classes and non-estimable scales remained explicitly unavailable.


183   The separate LDA diagnostic used original unstandardized CSP log features, saved coeffi-

184   cient/intercept and classes [0,1]:


185   s = f @ w + b; prediction = right if s > 0, otherwise left.


186   The intercept and zero threshold remained unchanged; support was neither centered nor used to

187   fit a score scale. With SD_s = SD_SOURCE40(s) using ddof=1, each half retained the balanced

188   true-class signed margin


189   M_h = (mean(s|right,h) − mean(s|left,h)) / (2*SD_s).


190   The factor of one half is part of this score-margin definition. Separate right margin mean(s|right,h)/SD_s,

191   left margin −mean(s|left,h)/SD_s, score location, recalls, predicted proportions, BA and col-

192   lapse were also saved. The vector-axis contrast and LDA margin are distinct measurements.

193   Neither is a calibrated probability or a validated neural-information measure. A positive half-

194   product can arise from two negative margins, and a positive class contrast can coexist with both

195   classes on one side of the frozen threshold.

228   S7.     Exported cue windows and unresolved within-trial task

229   phases

230   The released intervals are cue-anchored exported task-label windows, rather than five seconds

231   certified to contain uninterrupted motor imagery. Three questions are distinct: whether all samples

232   of an interval are available, whether its start corresponds to the intended marker, and whether its

233   complete span lies within active imagery or feedback. The first is established for the 7,156 included

234   intervals. The documented cue convention supports the second at the derivative/export level. The

235   third remains unresolved at trial level.


236   The original authors’ codebook distinguishes class cue markers 769/770 from subsequent task-start

237   markers 7691/7701 [1]. The preserved official MOABB Kumar adapter maps the former pair to

238   left/right hand and declares an interval of [0,5] seconds. NEMAR identifies MOABB conversion

239   and preserves corresponding onset/sample/class rows. Its metadata reports MOABB 1.5.0dev0,

240   but the exact historical converter commit/options and original GDF marker arrays have not been



                                                        12
241   independently replayed. A current adapter is evidence of the documented convention, not proof of

242   every historical conversion operation.


243   The original Methods describe one-second fixation, a 1.5-second class cue, then a five-second of-

244   fline task period or online feedback lasting at most seven seconds; a two-second result display and

245   1.5-second rest follow [2]. Online evidence-threshold crossing or timeout can terminate the active

246   period. Consequently, a nominal five-second interval beginning at the class cue contains cue pre-

247   sentation and at most the following 3.5 seconds of the task phase, assuming the described phase

248   schedule. Earlier online termination may introduce post-termination signal, but its presence and

249   duration cannot be assigned to individual intervals from the retained labels. We do not claim that

250   every online interval includes result display or rest.


251   The source codebook describes 32766 as recording boundary triggers and 1000 as trial/rest start.

252   For 7692/7702 it describes offline trial end and online timeout/incorrect-hit events. The online

253   7693/7703 description is literally “Cue Left/Right Delivered”; we do not silently reinterpret it as

254   a correct hit. The authors’ versioned task-bounded extraction function starts at 7691/7701 and ter-

255   minates at the subsequent 7692/7702 or 7693/7703. This supports the distinction between their

256   task-period markers and the class-cue export, without equating either annotation convention with

257   evidence of sustained imagery physiology.


258   The 360 saved event tables contain onset, duration, trial_type, value and sample, but not the

259   complete within-trial marker sequence. Each of the 44 partial intervals is the twentieth and final

260   retained class event of an online recording and ends at its previously audited EDF sample boundary.

261   Their identities, lengths and exclusion reasons remain unchanged. This demonstrates recording-

262   boundary truncation of the stored interval, not 44 verified early task terminations, and does not

263   by itself establish an indexing error. No retained interval overlaps the next retained class-event

264   onset; absence of this overlap still cannot demonstrate continuous imagery within a window.

333   S8.2. Class eligibility and aggregation

334   Both all_included and unflagged subsets retain rows for each participant, method, seed and

335   each of six sessions, plus separately labeled target-pooled and all-query-pooled scopes. Counts in-

336   clude true left/right, predicted left/right, all confusion cells and single-class prediction flags. Empty

337   and single-true-class groups remain present with undefined BA and an explicit reason; unavailable

338   values are not set to zero or chance.


339   Mathematical BA requires at least one example of each true class. A group with fewer than five ex-

340   amples in either class is flagged as low-count descriptive data but is not deleted by a new minimum-

341   count rule. A group containing both true classes can have model class collapse; a zero-query group

342   has no defined collapse assessment. EEGNet BA is computed separately for every seed and then

343   averaged over all three within participant/session. Counts represent unique query identities, not

344   trials multiplied by seeds, and no voting ensemble is constructed.


345   The target endpoint requires all five target-session BAs and gives each equal weight. If any target

346   session is undefined, that participant’s equal-session endpoint is undefined; fewer sessions are never

347   silently averaged. Cohort means use the explicitly listed eligible participants. Source, target-pooled

348   and all-query-pooled endpoints have their own eligibility counts and cannot replace the primary

349   equal-session endpoint. Development 6, remaining 12 and secondary all 18 remain separate.


350   Two remaining-cohort identities lose their unflagged five-session endpoint for all four methods.

450   S11. Post-review exploratory paired sign-flip checks

451   These checks were introduced in response to review after the original results were known. A com-

452   putation plan and a four-comparison Holm addendum were recorded before executing them; this

453   timing does not constitute preregistration or erase outcome exposure. Inputs are saved participant

454   endpoints, without EEG, weights, new predictions or changes to the original cohort and QC rules.


455   The fixed comparisons are full minus frozen, full minus LDA-only, and LDA-only minus frozen in

456   the same 12-person primary cohort, plus full minus frozen in the ten participants already eligible

457   for the unflagged sensitivity. For paired differences 𝑑𝑖 in BA units, the statistic is |𝑛−1 ∑𝑖 𝑑𝑖 |. We

458   enumerate all 2𝑛 vectors 𝑠𝑖 ∈ {−1, 1} and count |𝑛−1 ∑𝑖 𝑠𝑖 𝑑𝑖 | at least as large as the observed

459   value, allowing an absolute boundary tolerance of 10−12 BA with zero relative tolerance. The

460   p value is this count divided by 2𝑛 ; no Monte Carlo seed or plus-one correction is used for full



                                                        22
461   enumeration.


462   The enumeration is exhaustive for the sign-flip reference distribution. Its inferential interpreta-

463   tion requires independent participant differences whose signs are exchangeable around zero, condi-

464   tional on their magnitudes. It is not a randomization test justified by assigned treatment labels, an

465   assumption-free test of a population mean, or evidence that the participant identifiers were indepen-

466   dently verified biological identities.


467   The four tail fractions are 80/4,096, 42/4,096, 1,070/4,096 and 78/1,024. The unadjusted values

468   are 0.01953125, 0.01025390625, 0.26123046875 and 0.076171875; Holm adjustment over exactly

469   this four-test family gives 0.05859375, 0.041015625, 0.26123046875 and 0.15234375. Adjustment

470   addresses this reported family only and cannot correct all prior analytical exposure. In particular,

471   the primary contrast is not newly declared confirmatory on the basis of its unadjusted value. The

472   sample standardized paired effect for that contrast is 𝑑𝑧 = 0.807128; percentage-point estimates

473   remain the principal effect measure.


474   Four assignments in the LDA-only-minus-frozen comparison lie at a floating-point tail boundary.

475   The declared tolerance includes them (1,070/4,096); strict comparison of the saved decimal strings

476   as exact fractions gives 1,066/4,096. Both counts are preserved in the diagnostics, with no change

477   to the rule after calculation. The difference has no material effect on its interpretation.


478   All original percentile intervals are retained verbatim. Existing CPU code already resampled paired

479   participants jointly; the QC scalar implementation uses the same seed, size and lexicographic order

480   for each relevant contrast. Recomputing with numeric instead of lexicographic participant order

481   can change finite Monte Carlo percentile endpoints slightly, which does not justify replacing the

482   saved results. The unflagged contrast illustrates why interval exclusion and a different paired test

483   are not interchangeable: its retained interval is [0.99, 11.24] points, whereas the sign-flip p value is

484   0.076171875.
