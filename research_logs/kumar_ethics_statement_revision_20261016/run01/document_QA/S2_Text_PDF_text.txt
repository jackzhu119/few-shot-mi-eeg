43   Reproducing saved numerical results

44   S1 Data provides the dictionary, member hashes and commands in REPRODUCE_SAVED_RESULTS.md.

45   The saved-prediction script accepts explicit portable input and fresh output paths. Its original public-

46   input replay reproduced all 13 scientific QC CSVs byte-for-byte. Separate implementations checked

47   identity joins, eligibility, seed averaging, session weighting, percentile intervals and leave-one-

48   participant-out values. These checks were AI-assisted and do not substitute for the author’s personal

49   review.


50   The package also contains all 2,871 saved epoch-history rows and 54 fit summaries. The original

51   archive review extracted training text and streamed binary members only for integrity hashing. All

52   54 JSON histories were complete; 37 empty console logs were not interpreted as evidence of zero

53   execution. Source/target scores, every seed and class-collapsed predictions remain reported.


54   The four figures use exact saved numerical inputs. Fig 4 retains 180 participant/session contrast cells

55   and all negative values. In this revision, Fig 1’s generic point-type legend is clarified as participant

56   points colored by method; its data are unchanged. Fig 4 already used a symmetric RdBu scale,

57   which is retained. The producer writes fresh outputs without refitting a model or changing original

58   bootstrap intervals. TIFF dimensions, 600 dpi, RGB/LZW encoding and hashes were checked. The

59   actual installed face is Liberation Sans; Arial/Times family compliance is not claimed.


60   The post-review paired tests are separately identified in S1 Text, S11. They read saved participant

61   differences, enumerate fixed sign assignments and report all four unadjusted and Holm-adjusted

62   results. Their rules were written after review and outcome exposure; they do not retroactively

63   become a preregistered analysis. Original intervals and results remain unchanged. The complete

64   audit includes numerical boundary diagnostics and the preserved initial assertion failure; no failed

65   scientific attempt or historical result was erased.
