# Ethics-statement legal and journal-policy source review

Reviewed on 2026-10-16 UTC. This is a source review, not legal certification,
an institutional decision or a journal decision. It introduces no new EEG
access, participant contact, experiment, training or query evaluation.
This public directory contains original review text and source metadata only;
the retrieved legislation/policy HTML and PDFs are not redistributed here.

## What Article 32 supports

The official [Chinese government text](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2023-02/28/content_5743658.htm)
identifies the joint notice as **国卫科教发〔2023〕4号**, dated 18 February 2023,
issued by the National Health Commission, Ministry of Education, Ministry of
Science and Technology and National Administration of Traditional Chinese
Medicine. Article 54 provides that it takes effect upon issuance. The date in
the government webpage URL is not a separate amendment date.

Article 32 provides that qualifying research **may be exempt from ethics
review**. Item (1) covers legally obtained public data, or observation of
public behaviour without interference. The opening conditions also apply:
no harm to the human body and no involvement of sensitive personal information
or commercial interests. Public accessibility alone does not establish all
these conditions. Item (2), concerning anonymized information, is a separate
listed route; coded participant IDs do not by themselves demonstrate legal
anonymization.

The operative Chinese wording is “可以免除伦理审查”. It permits an exemption
when applicable; it is not an unconditional statement that every public-data
analysis needs no further review. Article 32 does not specify a universal new
written IRB form, reference number or procedure for every such exemption.
Article 5 retains the institution's management responsibility. Neither a
universal requirement for another new written IRB decision nor an unrestricted
right of self-exemption is established by this wording.

The English title in `china2023ethics` is an **unofficial translation from
Chinese**, prepared for the bibliography. The English rendering of the notice
identifier is a transcription; the official Chinese identifier is given above.
No official English version of the measures is asserted.

## Current text and later instruments: bounded finding

Targeted searches of national health, government, education and science
ministry sources did not identify an official amendment or repeal of the
2023 measures or Article 32 by the review date. This is a bounded search
result, not an exhaustive certification of every local rule or institutional
procedure.

Two later sources were checked:

- The science ministry's [2023 trial measures for science and technology ethics review](https://www.most.gov.cn/xxgk/xinxifenlei/fdzdgknr/fgzc/gfxwj/gfxwj2023/202310/t20231008_188309.html),
  effective 1 December 2023, retain qualifying sector-specific provisions in
  Article 54. The passages inspected do not repeal Article 32 of the health
  measures.
- The National Health Commission's [2026 research-integrity work plan](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/qjjys/c100015/202602/bda1529ba6374c089f70c2a57d74e0a4.shtml),
  also [reproduced on a local government website](https://www.jyg.gov.cn/wjw/zwxxgk/zc/zcfg/art/2026/art_142708fed0ce40d8983b7c7980776927.html),
  continues to invoke the measures. This supports continuing use of the
  framework, without independently proving that no later provision exists.

The clinical-research/clinical-translation regime for biomedical new
technologies is not used as the legal basis for this offline secondary
analysis of public EEG. No clinical approval or registration is invented.
Nor does this review assert that the 2023 text expressly repealed the
different 2016 biomedical-research measures.

## Institutional account and its provenance

The author reports that the university's research administration office
confirmed on **16 October 2026** that Article 32, item (1), applies and that
the secondary analysis is exempt from additional ethics review; no reference
number was issued. The university is understood to be Chongqing Medical

This is an **author-reported institutional confirmation**. No original
institutional document was independently examined in this source review.
It must not be described as independently verified IRB approval, assigned a
fabricated number, or called prior approval. The author's authorization to
revise the manuscript is a different source of authority from the reported
institutional confirmation, even when both are dated 16 October 2026.
The original UT Austin approval and consent continue
to describe the original data collection separately.

This review independently verifies the cited rules; it does not separately
certify the dataset's privacy status, every exemption condition or the
office's internal delegation. Those factual questions are distinct from
whether the exemption provision exists.

## What PLOS ONE permits

Current official [Submission Guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines)
allow authors who did not obtain approval to provide a detailed explanation
of why it was not needed. [Human Subjects Research](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/human-subjects-research)
recognizes exemption under applicable IRB/ethics regulations. These pages
do not establish a universal requirement for a new written IRB decision
for every public-data secondary analysis.

Article 32 and the author's reported institutional confirmation can therefore
be stated as the applicable basis, with the source, scope and provenance
made clear. They do not guarantee editorial acceptance:
[Criteria for Publication](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication)
retains journal judgement and allows standards more stringent than local
standards. Exemption from review is not interchangeable with IRB approval
or a waiver of informed consent.
