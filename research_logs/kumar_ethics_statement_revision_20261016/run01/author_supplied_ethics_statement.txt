Ethics statement

This study was a secondary analysis of publicly available EEG data from the Kumar2024 dataset, obtained from NEMAR (nm000177, version 1.0.5). Records were provided under coded participant identifiers. No new participants were recruited or contacted, no interventions were performed, and no attempts were made to re-identify participants.

The original study reported ethical approval from the University of Texas at Austin (protocol 2020-03-0073) and written informed consent obtained before data collection. These statements refer to the original data collection.

No additional ethical review was required for the present secondary analysis under Article 32(1) of China’s Measures for Ethical Review of Life Science and Medical Research Involving Human Participants (2023). The analysis used lawfully obtained publicly available data, caused no harm to participants, and did not involve sensitive personal information or commercial interests.
