"Basketball Motor Observation and Motor Imagery EEG Dataset"
"====================================="
""
"This dataset contains EEG recordings from 35 healthy participants performing 3 basketball-related motor observation and motor imagery tasks."
""
"Tasks:"
"1. dribble - Single-handed dribbling task with 5 conditions"
"2. ballpass - Two-handed ball passing task with 4 conditions"
"3. passing - Two-person passing task with 4 combined conditions (MOMO/MOMI/MIMO/MIMI) and 4 PLV conditions (observe/imagine opponent→self/self→opponent)"
""
"Data includes:"
"- Raw EEG data"
"- Preprocessed data"
"- PLV functional connectivity results"
""
"Recorded with ANT Neuro system (64 channels, 1000Hz sampling rate)."
""
"For more details, see the dataset_description.json and participants.json files."
""
"This dataset follows the BIDS 1.8.0 standard."
