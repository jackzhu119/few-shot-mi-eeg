Draft cover letter: for author review; not sent Dear PLOS ONE Editors, Please consider the Research Article “Longitudinal motor-imagery EEG decoding: Frozen baselines, super- vised recalibration, and task-measurement reliability.” This secondary analysis compares decoding updates in 18 participant records across six sessions of Ku- mar2024. The acquisition study investigated online covariance recentering and Riemannian class-prototype updating, with a separate pseudo-online CSP extension. Our analysis distinguishes classifier-only from full CSP/LDA refitting under the same 19/20-label current-session support budget and examines whether avail- able task measurements justify individual update decisions. The predefined primary comparison in 12 participants favored full refitting over frozen decoding by 7.42 percentage points (95% participant-bootstrap interval 2.49–12.48). The secondary same-label-budget com- parison favored full refitting over LDA-only updating by 5.47 points (2.17–8.91). All neural seeds, negative outcomes and class-collapsed predictions are retained. Weak EEGNet source-query performance limits inter- pretation of its later target result. QC sensitivity, incomplete class coverage, cue-window phase uncertainty and prior test exposure are reported explicitly. The additional paired tests were introduced after review and are labeled exploratory, with multiplicity shown.
